- In stage 1, you would ask for the house key of each person who wants to buy milk, go into their house, and leave the milk in the fridge. If someone changed the lock or moved, it would be chaos.

- In stage 2, the milkman passes by your house, shouts milk, and you go and get it. It's better than leaving milk in the fridge, but you have to know the person by name to call them (or if they move, you'll have to work to get to know the new owner). Better than stage 1, but not perfect.

- In stage 3, you put up a sign on your store and whoever wants to come and buy milk. You don't even need to know who your customers are.

The trade-off is that in stage 3 you need to build a place and the sign, but selling to 10 people is the same amount of work as selling to 100.
